\textit{Notes:} This table reports ordinary least squares estimates of $\beta_k$ for predictive regressions of cash-flow growth in each of the five years following the forecast origin,
\[g^{year}_{t,k}=\alpha_k+\beta_k z_{t-1}^{EE}+\gamma_k g_t^-+\varepsilon_{t,k},
\qquad k=1,\ldots,5,\]
where $g^{year}_{t,k}=100\log(C_{t+12k}/C_{t+12(k-1)})$ and $C_t$ is nominal trailing-twelve-month S\&P 500 earnings or dividends. The predictor $z_{t-1}^{EE}$ is standardized EE mobility lagged one month, and $g_t^-=100\log(C_t/C_{t-12})$ is past annual growth. The EE-only specification omits $g_t^-$. Both models and all five future years use the same 300 forecast origins, January 1996--December 2020. EE and the past-growth control remain measured at the original origin rather than being updated for each future year. Coefficients are log-growth percentage points per one-standard-deviation increase in EE. Newey--West standard errors use 60 monthly lags for every specification; the $p$ column and coefficient stars use two-sided Student-$t$ probabilities. $R^2$ is reported in percent. $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. Appendix \ref{app:cash-long} provides the common-sample construction.\par\end{minipage}
