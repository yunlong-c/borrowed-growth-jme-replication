\textit{Notes:} This table reports ordinary least squares estimates of $\beta$ for the contemporaneous regression
\[z_t^Z=\alpha+\beta z_t^{EE}+\varepsilon_t,\]
where $z_t^{EE}$ and $z_t^Z$ denote EE mobility and the financial condition in the first column, respectively, standardized over March 1996--December 2025 and aligned by economic month. The forward-earnings-yield premium is the S\&P 500 forward earnings yield less the expected real yield on ten-year Treasury securities. BEX economic uncertainty is the fitted conditional variance of industrial-production growth from \citet{BEX2022}. The final row uses the month-end Cboe SKEW index. Coefficients measure outcome standard deviations per one-standard-deviation increase in EE. The $t_{NW}$ column reports Newey--West statistics with 12 monthly lags; $p$ and stars use two-sided Student-$t$ probabilities. $R^2$ is reported in percent, and each regression contains 358 observations. $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.
