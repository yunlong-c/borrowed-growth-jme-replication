\textit{Notes:} This table reports in-sample estimation results and out-of-sample forecast performance for the financial and macroeconomic predictors. The predictive regression is
$y_{t,h}=\alpha_h+\beta_h z_{X,t}+\varepsilon_{t,h},$
where $y_{t,h}=(100/h)\sum_{j=1}^{h}r^e_{t+j}$ is the average monthly aggregate log excess return over the subsequent $h$ months, expressed in percentage points, and $z_{X,t}$ is the standardized predictor identified in the first column. Inflation uses the preceding month's observation; the other predictors use month $t$. The horizon columns correspond to $h=1,3,6,12$ months. Slope coefficients measure percentage-point changes in average monthly returns per one-standard-deviation increase in the predictor. Out-of-sample targets begin in January 2010, and all return windows end by December 2025. $R^2_{OS}$ measures the reduction in mean squared forecast error relative to the historical-average forecast; both $R^2$ measures are reported in percent. Parentheses below in-sample coefficients and out-of-sample $R^2_{OS}$ report Newey--West and Clark--West statistics, respectively. In-sample significance uses the two-sided studentized monthly wild bootstrap; out-of-sample significance uses the one-sided Clark--West test. $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. Appendix \ref{app:data-definitions} and \ref{app:B-is}--\ref{app:B-oos} provide variable definitions and estimation details.\par\end{minipage}
