\textit{Notes:} This table reports annualized CER gains, Sharpe ratios, and turnover for the EE allocation strategy and its matched historical-average (HA) benchmark under alternative investment specifications. The three column groups report the respective performance measures over holding periods of $h=1,3,6,12$ months. The baseline investor has risk aversion three, equity weights constrained to $[-0.5,1.5]$, and a variance estimate based on the most recent 120 completed same-horizon returns. Alternative specifications change risk aversion in both allocation and CER evaluation, the variance-estimation window, evaluation or training start dates, the rolling estimation window, or forecast restrictions, as indicated by the row labels. The simple-return check retains the baseline allocation because investment forecasts already use compounded simple excess returns. The nonnegative-forecast specification floors the compounded simple excess-return forecast at zero; these investment forecasts are positive throughout the evaluation sample, so the allocation is unchanged. CER gains are measured in percentage points per year relative to HA under the same implementation. In the transaction-cost specifications, both strategies incur the indicated one-way cost on drift-adjusted equity trades, including the initial allocation; no terminal liquidation cost is imposed. Other specifications are before transaction costs. Turnover is the average absolute equity trade as a fraction of pretrade wealth, annualized by $12/h$ and reported in portfolio multiples. Sharpe ratios use excess portfolio returns and are annualized by $\sqrt{12/h}$. Appendix \ref{app:B-allocation}--\ref{app:B-robust} provide the investment rules.\par\end{minipage}
