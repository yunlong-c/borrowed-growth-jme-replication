\textit{Notes:} This table reports the out-of-sample performance of combinations of the EE return forecast and the forecast based on the companion predictor in the first column. Column group A assigns one-half of the combination weight to each forecast. Column group B uses weights proportional to the inverse of discounted mean squared forecast errors (DMSFE), with a discount factor of 0.75 per completed paired forecast observation. DMSFE weights are updated recursively using completed forecast errors; equal weights are used until 12 paired errors have been observed. Forecasts target average monthly log excess returns over $h=1,3,6,12$ months. Each combination is evaluated against the historical-average forecast on common forecast dates. $R^2_{OS}$ is the percentage reduction in mean squared forecast error, and the $t_{CW}$ columns report the Clark--West statistic. $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively, based on the one-sided Clark--West test. Appendix \ref{app:B-combination} provides the recursive weighting rules.\par\end{minipage}
