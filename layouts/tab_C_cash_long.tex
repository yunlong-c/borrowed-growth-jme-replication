\textit{Notes:} This table reports ordinary least squares estimates of $\beta_h$ for long-horizon cash-flow predictive regressions
\[g_{t,h}^{w}=\alpha_h+\beta_h z_{t-1}^{EE}+\gamma_h g_t^-+\varepsilon_{t,h},\]
where $C_t$ is nominal trailing-twelve-month S\&P 500 earnings or dividends, $z_{t-1}^{EE}$ is standardized EE mobility lagged one month, and $g_t^-=100\log(C_t/C_{t-12})$. The cumulative-growth target is $g_{t,h}^{cum}=100\log(C_{t+h}/C_t)$, while the after-first-year target is $g_{t,h}^{post}=100\log(C_{t+h}/C_{t+12})$. In both cases, $h=24,36,48,60$ denotes the endpoint in months from the original forecast origin. The EE-only model omits past growth; the controlled model includes it. Both specifications use common valid origins within each outcome, window, and horizon. The reported EE coefficient is in cumulative log-growth percentage points per one-standard-deviation increase in mobility. The $t_{NW}$ columns use Newey--West standard errors with $h$ monthly lags for both target definitions, and $p$ reports two-sided Student-$t$ probabilities. $R^2$ is reported in percent, and $N$ is the number of observations. $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. Appendix \ref{app:cash-long} provides the sample and inference details.\par\end{minipage}
