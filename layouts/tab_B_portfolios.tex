\textit{Notes:} This table reports annualized certainty-equivalent return (CER) gains in the left column group and annualized Sharpe ratios in the right column group for portfolios based on alternative return forecasts. A mean--variance investor allocates between the stock market and risk-free bills using forecasts of compounded simple excess returns over the $h$-month holding period. Risk aversion is three, and equity weights are constrained to $[-0.5,1.5]$. All forecast-based strategies use the same variance estimate from the most recent 120 completed, monthly overlapping, same-horizon returns. CER gains are measured in percentage points per year relative to the matched historical-average (HA) strategy, which forecasts the historical mean of the same holding-period return target. Sharpe ratios use portfolio returns in excess of bills. Buy-and-hold maintains an equity weight of one. Portfolios rebalance every $h$ months over January 2010--December 2025. CERs and Sharpe ratios are annualized by $12/h$ and $\sqrt{12/h}$, respectively, and returns are before transaction costs. A missing current comparison signal is replaced by the HA forecast. Appendix \ref{app:B-allocation} provides the allocation rules.\par\end{minipage}
