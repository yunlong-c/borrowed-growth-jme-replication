\textit{Notes:} This table reports ordinary least squares estimates for cash-flow predictive regressions of the form
\[g_{t,h}=\alpha_h+\beta_h z_{t-1}^{EE}+\gamma_h g_t^-+\varepsilon_{t,h},\]
where $g_{t,h}=100\log(C_{t+h}/C_t)$, $g_t^-=100\log(C_t/C_{t-12})$, and $C_t$ is nominal trailing-twelve-month S\&P 500 earnings or dividends. The predictor $z_{t-1}^{EE}$ is standardized EE mobility lagged one month. The EE-only model omits past growth, the past-growth-only model omits EE, and the EE + past growth model includes both. The reported slope is $\beta_h$ in the models containing EE and $\gamma_h$ in the past-growth-only model. EE slopes measure cumulative log-growth percentage points per one-standard-deviation increase in mobility; past-growth slopes are per percentage point of past growth. All three models use common valid origins within each outcome and horizon. The $t_{NW}$ columns report Newey--West statistics with 12 monthly lags. The $p$ columns and coefficient stars use two-sided paired moving-block bootstrap probabilities based on centered studentized statistics, with 12-month blocks and 20,000 replications. $R^2$ is reported in percent, and $N$ is the number of regression observations. $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels. Appendix \ref{app:cash-controls} provides the bootstrap and sample details.\par\end{minipage}
